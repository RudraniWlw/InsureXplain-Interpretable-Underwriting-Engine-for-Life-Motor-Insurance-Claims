3. Findings and recommendations

The model never sees protected attributes, but a feature that correlates with one can act as a proxy. Where a feature is flagged, underwriters should confirm it has a documented, actuarially justified link to claims, and consider capping its influence or testing the model without it. Re-run this audit after every retraining.

Method notes

Data are synthetic and generated with a known risk formula, including a young-driver by high-mileage interaction and a sports-car proxy for gender, so the audit has something real to find. SHAP values are in log-odds from TreeExplainer. A positive SHAP value is treated as an adverse factor. This is a teaching prototype and not legal advice.
